The soul of Rome,
in Glyfada.

At Pino, we bring the soul of Rome to the heart of the Athens Riviera. Founded by Luca Piscazzi and his wife Charlotte Luykx — Luca’s culinary journey began at the age of six in his grandmother’s kitchen just outside Rome.

With over 15 years of experience in Michelin-starred restaurants, Luca has always dreamed of sharing his favourite Roman dishes with his guests. Our restaurant captures the warmth, simplicity and vibrancy of Roman dining in a modern setting.
